### Extras

- Hidden Recipe Examples guide, opened with `/guideme open @p gmrecipes:examples gmrecipes:<mod_id>.md`, drawing one loaded recipe per recipe type a mod registers so gaps in coverage name themselves. A mod's page is hidden when that mod is absent. A recipe type whose mod never loads a recipe of its own, such as the Energized Smelter reading vanilla furnace recipes or Create's basin and conversion types, is drawn from a placeholder recipe labelled as one.
