# Create Big Cannons

Basin Melting shows the items or tags that go into the foundry basin on the left, the molten metal it yields on the right, and the heat the basin needs underneath. The munition recipes (cartridge assembly, fuzing, tracer application and removal, and their deployer variants) are drawn with the type's own title and icon.
