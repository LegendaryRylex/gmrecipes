# Apotheosis

Salvaging shows the item or rarity put into the table, with each result and its minimum and maximum count. Reforging shows the rarity material and sigils of rebirth spent, the tables that accept it and the level cost. Gem cutting lays out the gem, its second copy and the left and right materials around the table, with the purity step.

The sigil smithing recipes (malice, unnaming, withdrawal, supremacy, socketing, add sockets) and sized upgrades appear as smithing boxes with example items and the required addition. They share vanilla's smithing type, so they are not listed below.
