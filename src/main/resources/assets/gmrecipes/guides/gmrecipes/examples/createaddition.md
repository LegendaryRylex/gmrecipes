# Create Crafts & Additions

Rolling Mill recipes show the input and the rolled result. Tesla Coil charging recipes show the item, the result and the energy and maximum charge rate in FE. Liquid burning recipes show the fuel fluid, how long it burns in a Blaze Burner and whether it superheats it.
