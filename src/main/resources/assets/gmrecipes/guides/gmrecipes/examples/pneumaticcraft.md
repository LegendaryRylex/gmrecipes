# PneumaticCraft: Repressurized

One example for every PneumaticCraft: Repressurized recipe type loaded by this pack.

Thermopneumatic Processing Plant, Pressure Chamber, Fluid Mixer and Refinery recipes show their fluid and item slots with the required pressure in bar and the operating temperature range in degrees Celsius. Assembly Controller recipes show the input, the output, the program item and the machines it needs. Heat Frame Cooling, Explosion Crafting, Block Heat Properties and Amadron trades show their slots with the numbers that govern them: cooling threshold and bonus, material loss rate, heat values, and the vendor.
