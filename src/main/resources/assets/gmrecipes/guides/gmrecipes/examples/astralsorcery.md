# Astral Sorcery

Altar crafting shows the three by three grid, the relay slots when a recipe uses them, the extra thrown-in items and fluids, the lumen it needs, and its duration, night restriction and constellation requirements. Starlight transmutation, lightwell, infusion, lumen generation, lumen crystallization, liquid starlight and chalice interaction recipes show their inputs and outputs with times, multipliers and chances beside them. The two dye recipes use the plain crafting layout.
