# Farmer's Delight

Cooking pot recipes show up to six ingredients, the finished dish, the container it is served in, the cooking time and the experience it grants. Cutting board recipes show the required tool and the item being cut on the left, and every result on the right with its drop chance. The two crafting specials, food serving and dough, carry no data of their own and appear under the plain header.
