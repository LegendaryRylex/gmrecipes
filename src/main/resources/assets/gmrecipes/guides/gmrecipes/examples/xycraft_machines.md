# XyCraft Machines

Every XyCraft Machines recipe type loaded by this pack, one example each. Extractor recipes list the target block, the blocks or fluids around it, any catalyst, the faces it works from and the time. Processing machines such as the blender, refinery, centrifuge, crusher, squasher, ark melter and cryo chamber show their input and output slots with time, entropy and any research stage they need. Crusher outputs carry their chance. The buildings, isolator, ore tap, solidifier, atmospheric vacuum and fluid tank types show their slots with the numbers that apply.
