# Apothic Enchanting

Infusion recipes show the item put on the enchanting table, the item it becomes, and the Eterna, Quanta and Arcana it needs. Each stat shows its minimum, a range when the recipe also caps it, or the exact value. Quanta and Arcana are percentages. Recipes that keep the item's data use the same layout.
