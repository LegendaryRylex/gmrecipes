# Malum

Spirit Infusion, Spirit Repair, Soul Binding and Spirit Focusing show their spirits as counted slots on the left, the prime item in the middle, extra items beside it and the result on the right. Spirit Repair adds the durability restored, and Spirit Focusing the time and durability cost. The Unchained Rite, Runeworking and the Weeping Well show their inputs, an arrow and the result.
