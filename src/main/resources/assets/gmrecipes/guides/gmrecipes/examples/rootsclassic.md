# Roots Classic

Altar rituals list the items placed on the altar, the incense burned in the surrounding braziers, and the standing stone level the ritual needs. A ritual that crafts an item shows its result; any other ritual names its effect instead.

Mortar components list the ingredients ground together and the spell powder they make, with the spell effect it carries above.
