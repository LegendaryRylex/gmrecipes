# Eternal Starlight

One example of each Eternal Starlight recipe type loaded by this pack. Alloy Furnace recipes show the 3x3 ingredient grid, the main and byproduct outputs with their roll ranges, and the burn time. Drying Rack recipes show the input, the campfire that must burn below, the result and the drying time. Geyser Smoking recipes show the item count consumed and the item produced. The special crafting recipes (mana crystals, tool modification, accessory combination) appear under their own headings.
