# Reliquary Reincarnations

The Infernal Tear's experience table shows each absorbable item with the experience points it is worth and the player level those points amount to. Alkahestry, potion bullet, mob charm and fragment recipes are crafting-table recipes and appear in the ordinary crafting grid.
