# Ender IO

Ender IO machines draw their inputs on the left and outputs on the right, with the energy each recipe takes underneath. The Enchanter shows its book and level cost at every level, the Sag Mill gives the chance of each output, slicing shows the tools it wears down, soul binding names the mob it takes, and fire crafting and weather changes name the dimensions and weather they apply to.
