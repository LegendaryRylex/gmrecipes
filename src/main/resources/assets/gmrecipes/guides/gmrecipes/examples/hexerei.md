# Hexerei

Hexerei recipes as the mod shows them: the Mixing Cauldron ring of up to eight items with its fluid, heat source and moon phase, the Candle Dipper with dip and drying times, the Herb Drying Rack, the Pestle and Mortar with stacked ingredient counts, and bottling and filling of the cauldron. Candle and crafting specials fall back to the standard view.
