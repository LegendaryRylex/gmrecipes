# Oritech

Oritech machines draw their recipes as inputs on the left and outputs on the right, with items and fluids in the same grid. The line under each recipe gives its duration in ticks and seconds. Generators and fuel burners list what they consume with the burn time underneath, and the Particle Accelerator shows the minimum collision energy instead of a duration.
