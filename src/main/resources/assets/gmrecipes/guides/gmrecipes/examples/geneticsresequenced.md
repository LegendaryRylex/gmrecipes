# Genetics: Resequenced

Incubator recipes show the top and bottom input slots on the left and the result on the right. Cell Growth, Mutation, Substrate and Viral Agents potions sit in the bottom slot. GMO recipes list the success and failure cells with their chances, and recipes that need the Advanced Incubator at low temperature carry a temperature line under the box. The two Anti-Plasmid crafting recipes use the plain crafting layout.
