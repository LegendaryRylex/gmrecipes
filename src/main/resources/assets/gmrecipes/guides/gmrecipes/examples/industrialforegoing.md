# Industrial Foregoing

Dissolution Chamber recipes lay the eight ring inputs out around the input fluid, with the item and fluid outputs, processing time and total energy beside them. Laser Drill recipes show the lens, the item or fluid it yields, and for every rarity entry the Y range, weight and any dimension or biome filters. Fluid Extractor recipes show the log, the block it leaves behind, the latex output and break chance. StoneWork Generation shows the water and lava needed and what is consumed, and StoneWork Crushing shows the input and its crushed result.
